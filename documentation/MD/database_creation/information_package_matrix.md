## Step 2: Information Package Diagram & Data Warehouse Requirements Matrix

### Executive Overview & Analytical Scope
The **Information Package Diagram** defines the conceptual requirements for the AOCS Data Warehouse prior to building the Star Schema. In accordance with the **Ralph Kimball Data Warehouse Lifecycle**, this document outlines the analytical subject area, conformed dimensions, drill-down hierarchies, and quantitative fact metrics (KPIs).

To prevent information loss across the **16 operational entities** defined in the Peter Chen ERD, this model establishes **10 Dedicated Dimensions** ensuring 100% coverage of flight, infrastructure, personnel, passenger, and ground logistics data.

---

## SECTION 1: BUSINESS SUBJECT AREA & GRAIN

* **Subject Area**: `Flight Turnaround & Operational Performance Analytics`
* **Business Objective**: Evaluate operational efficiency, quantify turnaround delays, measure ground crew productivity, track resource utilization (gates/runways), and optimize fuel/cargo throughput.
* **Fact Table Grain**: **Atomic Grain — One record per individual flight turnaround event.**

## SECTION 3: FACT METRICS & MEASURES (QUANTITATIVE KPIS)

The measures stored at the center of the Information Package quantify turnaround performance:

### 1. Turnaround Duration Metrics
* **`planned_turnaround_mins`**: Scheduled target duration for turnaround.
* **`actual_turnaround_mins`**: Realized elapsed time from gate arrival to pushback.
* **`turnaround_variance_mins`**: Difference (`actual_turnaround_mins - planned_turnaround_mins`).
* **`turnaround_efficiency_pct`**: Ratio of target to actual turnaround (`(planned / actual) * 100`).

### 2. Delay & Bottleneck Metrics
* **`total_delay_mins`**: Total accumulated delay time for the flight.
* **`ground_delay_mins`**: Portion of delay incurred during ground turnaround.
* **`air_delay_mins`**: Portion of delay incurred during airborne holding/approach.
* **`delay_incident_count`**: Count of distinct logged delay incidents (`DELAY_LOGS`).

### 3. Logistics & Resource Volume Metrics
* **`passenger_count`**: Total boarded passengers on the flight.
* **`lounge_visit_count`**: Total executive lounge visits generated by the flight passengers.
* **`baggage_units_processed`**: Total checked luggage items delivered to carousels.
* **`cargo_weight_kg`**: Total weight of cargo containers loaded.
* **`fuel_delivered_liters`**: Total volume of aviation fuel pumped.

### 4. Staff & Task Execution Metrics
* **`total_tasks_assigned`**: Count of ground turnaround sub-tasks created.
* **`tasks_completed_on_time`**: Count of sub-tasks finished within SLA window.
* **`task_delay_duration_mins`**: Cumulative delay time across sub-tasks.
